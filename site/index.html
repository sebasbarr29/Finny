How it works

From spreadsheet to game plan in four steps.

No accounting degree needed. Bring the files you already have.

  1. Upload your numbers

    Drop in a monthly P&L or a bank export.

    .csvP&Lbank export

  2. Get your report

    Finny explains what changed, why it matters, and what to do next, in plain English or Spanish.

  3. Ask follow-ups

    Ask Finny questions about your results, like why costs jumped or which month was strongest.

  4. Act on a plan

    Turn insights into a clear monthly action plan and track your key numbers over time.
